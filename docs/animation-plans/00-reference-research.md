# Reference Research

## How references should be used
References are for pacing, hierarchy, restraint, and interaction quality. They should not be copied visually or mechanically. The app is a life operating system, so motion must support repeated work, reflection, planning, and retrieval.

## GSAP Showcase
Borrow:
- Timeline rhythm for hero and section entrances.
- Scroll-triggered reveals that clarify story structure.
- Text reveal polish on true hero moments only.
- Strong hierarchy and restrained sequencing.

Avoid:
- Agency-portfolio chaos.
- Long intro loaders.
- Continuous decorative cursor effects.

## GSAP Demo Hub patterns
Useful isolated patterns:
- Magnetic CTA feedback for primary actions.
- Batched reveal for repeated cards.
- Flip for active tabs, nav indicators, and reordered cards.
- Animated counters only for high-value metrics.

## Awwwards-style motion
Borrow:
- Premium landing-page pacing.
- Editorial entrance timing.
- Image/card reveal craft.

Avoid:
- Scroll-jacking.
- Overly theatrical page transitions for productivity pages.
- Effects that make controls feel slippery or unpredictable.

## Apple-style product storytelling
Borrow:
- One idea per section.
- Quiet sections between dramatic moments.
- Simple copy supported by precise reveal timing.

Avoid:
- Heavy pinned scenes in pages where users need to edit, search, or compare data.
